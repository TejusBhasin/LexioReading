Lexio is an all-in-one AI-powered reading companion designed to transform your reading habits. Whether you're an avid reader tracking thousands of pages or a casual learner looking for your next favorite book, Lexio provides the tools to organize, analyze, and enrich your literary journey.

Core Features
Personalized Discovery: AI-driven recommendations tailored to your unique tastes, reading history, and preferred genres.
Comprehensive Library: Manage your "Want to Read," "Reading," and "Finished" lists with detailed metadata.
Reading Logs & Streaks: Build consistent habits by logging sessions, tracking time spent, and maintaining daily reading streaks.
Social & Community: Join reading clubs, participate in forum discussions, and share thoughts with a global community of readers.
AI Book Companion: Engage in deep discussions about books, ask for recommendations, or explore themes with our integrated AI chat.
Reading Insights: View your annual reading statistics and achievements through our "Wrapped" feature.
School Integration: Dedicated environments for classrooms and teachers to track reading progress with administrative tools.
Tech Stack
Lexio is built on Base44, leveraging a robust modern stack:

Frontend: React, Tailwind CSS, and Shadcn UI.
Backend: Base44 Backend-as-a-Service, featuring real-time entity synchronization and serverless functions.
Intelligence: Integrated LLMs for personalized recommendations, content moderation, and reading activity analysis.
Getting Started
Authentication: Secure user management with built-in OAuth support.
Onboarding: Personalized setup tour to define genre preferences and content filters.
Discovery: Access the Discover page to search across millions of titles via the Google Books API.
Community: Dive into forums and clubs to connect with fellow readers.
