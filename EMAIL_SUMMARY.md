# Email: Major Platform Fixes & Improvements

**Subject:** Lexio Platform — Major Safety, Privacy, and Admin Improvements Complete

---

Dear School Administrators and Partners,

I'm writing to share a comprehensive update on the major improvements we've made to the Lexio platform. Over the past several weeks, we've addressed critical safety, privacy, and administrative control issues to make Lexio more secure and school-friendly than ever.

## 1. Enhanced Student Safety & Moderation

**AI-Powered Safety Dashboard:** School admins now have a dedicated Security tab with real-time incident tracking, AI-powered priority sorting of reported content, and one-click access to ban, notify, or offboard students.

**Smarter Content Flagging:** Reported content is now intelligently routed — if your school has Content Isolation enabled, flagged content goes directly to your school admin team for review. Otherwise, it goes to Lexio's moderation team. This ensures the right people handle incidents at the right time.

**Anti-Cheating Safeguards:** Our AI chat assistant has been reinforced to strictly prohibit providing plot summaries, chapter recaps, or homework answers. The AI now redirects students to book recommendations and literary discussion rather than acting as a study shortcut.

**Spoiler-Free Guarantee:** All AI-generated book summaries are now reinforced to remain 100% spoiler-free, focusing only on tone and premise.

## 2. Student Lifecycle Management

**Student Offboarding:** School admins can now cleanly offboard students with a guided flow that preserves reading data while revoking school access. Offboarded students can continue using Lexio independently.

**Bulk Account Deletion:** Admins can now input a list of student emails to permanently delete accounts and all associated data — reading logs, reviews, library entries, vault content, posts, and comments — in a single operation with full confirmation steps.

**Student Data Export:** Before offboarding or at any time, admins can export a student's complete reading data as a JSON file for record-keeping or transfer.

**Ban System Improvements:** The ban system now enforces a 7-day maximum ban duration with a 14-day cooldown between bans, preventing over-punishment while maintaining accountability.

## 3. Privacy & Compliance

**Professional Terms & Privacy Policy:** We've replaced aggressive liability language with clear, professional, school-friendly terms. Our privacy documentation now includes:

- **Data Security & Storage:** Details on encryption (at rest and in transit), API key protection, and PIN-protected vault data
- **COPPA Compliance:** For students under 13, the school provides consent under COPPA's school authorization exception. We collect only minimal data, never use student data for advertising, and allow deletion requests at any time
- **Breach Response:** In the event of a confirmed data breach, we commit to investigating, containing, and notifying affected users and school administrators within 72 hours
- **FERPA Awareness:** Clear documentation of data access controls, offboarding procedures, and content isolation

## 4. School Administration Tools

**Semi-Admin Roles:** School admins can now delegate specific permissions to semi-admins — viewing member lists, managing restrictions, kicking members, and more — with granular control over what each semi-admin can do.

**Class Management:** Improved class creation, student assignment, and teacher delegation with per-class feature restrictions.

**School AI Analytics:** Ask natural-language questions about your students' reading data and get AI-generated insights, trends, and recommendations.

**Email Domain Auto-Join:** Submit your school's email domain for approval. Once approved, anyone who signs up with that domain automatically joins your school.

## 5. Search & Discovery Fixes

**Restored Book Search:** We fixed an issue where an overly aggressive word filter was blocking common book titles (like *New Kid*). Search now includes proper loading states, empty/error handling, and clear messaging.

---

These improvements reflect our commitment to making Lexio a safe, compliant, and powerful reading platform for schools. We welcome your feedback and suggestions for future improvements.

If you have any questions or need assistance with any of these features, please reach out through the in-app Support page or contact your Lexio representative.
